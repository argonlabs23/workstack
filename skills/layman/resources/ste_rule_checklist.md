# ASD-STE100 Compliance Verification Checklist

Use this checklist to verify that rewritten text strictly complies with the ASD-STE100 standard.

### 1. Vocabulary & Word Choice
- [ ] Are all verbs restricted to approved dictionary meanings?
- [ ] Are unapproved verbs like `utilize`, `commence`, `terminate`, `obtain`, `depress`, or `ascertain` replaced?
- [ ] Are verbose prepositions (`in order to`, `prior to`, `subsequent to`, `due to the fact that`) simplified to `to`, `before`, `after`, `because`?
- [ ] Is each word used in only one part of speech?
- [ ] Are Technical Names (TNs) consistent and clear?

### 2. Sentence Length & Structure
- [ ] Are procedural sentences (instructions) 20 words or fewer?
- [ ] Are descriptive sentences (explanations) 25 words or fewer?
- [ ] Does each sentence convey only ONE instruction or ONE main idea?
- [ ] Are conditional clauses placed BEFORE the action clause? ("If X, do Y.")

### 3. Voice & Tenses
- [ ] Is the active voice used throughout?
- [ ] Are passive constructions ("was tested by", "must be closed") eliminated?
- [ ] Are verb tenses restricted to simple present, simple past, simple future, or infinitive?
- [ ] Are gerunds (`-ing` words as nouns) avoided?

### 4. Nouns & Noun Clusters
- [ ] Are noun clusters limited to a maximum of 3 words?
- [ ] Are complex noun phrases clarified with hyphens or prepositions?

### 5. Paragraphs & Layout
- [ ] Are paragraphs limited to 6 sentences or fewer?
- [ ] Does each paragraph focus on a single topic?
- [ ] Are lists vertically formatted when containing 3 or more items?
