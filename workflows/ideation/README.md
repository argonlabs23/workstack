# 🚀 Ideation & Validation Workflow Suite

The **Workstak Ideation Suite** is a set of AI prompts. They help founders test new business ideas before spending time or money. These prompts challenge your assumptions, analyze competitors, calculate market size, review customer interviews, and spot common traps.

---

## 🛠️ Agents Overview

### 1. ⚡ [Idea Pressure-Tester](./pressure-test.md)
* **Filename:** [`pressure-test.md`](./pressure-test.md)
* **What it does:** Tests your raw idea to find weak spots and false assumptions. It turns vague thoughts into clear, testable statements. It also gives you interview questions to test your idea with real people.

---

### 2. ⚔️ [Competitor Analyst](./competitor-analysis.md)
* **Filename:** [`competitor-analysis.md`](./competitor-analysis.md)
* **What it does:** Shows the real strengths of your competitors. It maps direct rivals, big tech companies, and simple alternatives like spreadsheets. It helps you see why competitors can win so you can build real advantages.

---

### 3. 📊 [Market Research Analyst](./market-research.md)
* **Filename:** [`market-research.md`](./market-research.md)
* **What it does:** Finds real complaints in online reviews for existing software. It calculates practical bottom-up market size numbers instead of wild guesses. It also maps who signs the check versus who uses the tool.

---

### 4. 🌊 [Trends Analyst](./trends-analysis.md)
* **Filename:** [`trends-analysis.md`](./trends-analysis.md)
* **What it does:** Evaluates outside forces over the next two years. It checks new laws, changing technology, and customer habits. It shows whether each trend helps your product grow or creates a risk.

---

### 5. 🧪 [Customer Discovery Analyst](./customer-discovery.md)
* **Filename:** [`customer-discovery.md`](./customer-discovery.md)
* **What it does:** Helps you talk to customers and understand their real needs. It fixes bad interview questions so you do not get fake praise. It also reviews interview notes to find honest customer proof.

---

### 6. 🧩 [Idea Pattern Synthesizer](./pattern-recognition.md)
* **Filename:** [`pattern-recognition.md`](./pattern-recognition.md)
* **What it does:** Compares two or more ideas at the same time. It finds shared advantages and warns you about common startup traps. It then ranks your ideas by their chances of success.

---

## 🎯 Recommended Workflow Steps

1. **Compare Ideas:** Run [`pattern-recognition.md`](./pattern-recognition.md) to compare concepts and avoid common startup traps.
2. **Test Your Hypothesis:** Run [`pressure-test.md`](./pressure-test.md) to define clear success and failure criteria.
3. **Analyze Competitors:** Run [`competitor-analysis.md`](./competitor-analysis.md) to find the true strengths of your rivals.
4. **Size Your Market:** Run [`market-research.md`](./market-research.md) to calculate realistic revenue potential and identify buyers.
5. **Evaluate Macro Trends:** Run [`trends-analysis.md`](./trends-analysis.md) to find two-year market tailwinds and headwinds.
6. **Talk to Customers:** Run [`customer-discovery.md`](./customer-discovery.md) to write interview questions and analyze customer feedback.
