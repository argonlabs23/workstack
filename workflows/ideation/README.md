# 🚀 Ideation & Validation Workflow Suite

The **Workstak Ideation & Validation Workflow Suite** is a collection of specialized AI Red-Team agents designed to stress-test product hypotheses, deconstruct competitive landscapes, model bottom-up market dynamics, audit customer discovery interviews, and identify portfolio patterns before investing capital or engineering time.

---

## 🛠️ Agents Overview

### 1. ⚡ [Idea Pressure-Tester & Hypothesis Architect](./pressure-test.md)
* **Filename:** [`pressure-test.md`](./pressure-test.md)
* **What it does:** Stress-tests raw business ideas by acting as an unsparing Red-Team evaluator. It sharpens vague concepts into formal, falsifiable hypotheses, maps implicit "Leaps of Faith" across Desirability/Viability/Feasibility/Adoption, constructs fierce counter-arguments, and designs non-leading *Mom Test* discovery protocols.

---

### 2. ⚔️ [Competitor Steel-Titan & Landscape Strategist](./competitor-analysis.md)
* **Filename:** [`competitor-analysis.md`](./competitor-analysis.md)
* **What it does:** Eliminates confirmation bias by refusing to attack competitor strawmen. It maps your competitive ecosystem across 4 distinct tiers (*Direct Rivals, Indirect/Status Quo, Platform Incumbents, and Adjacent Players*) and articulates the strongest non-trivial structural arguments for why competitors will win and why your product faces existential risk.

---

### 3. 📊 [Market Research & Buyer Landscape Analyst](./market-research.md)
* **Filename:** [`market-research.md`](./market-research.md)
* **What it does:** Synthesizes customer reviews (G2, Capterra, Reddit) to surface unmet complaints in existing solutions. It replaces top-down market estimates with bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM financial models, classifies market lifecycle maturity (*Expanding, Consolidating, Mature, Decaying*), and maps the procurement hierarchy (*Economic Buyer vs. Decision Maker vs. Champion vs. End User*).

---

### 4. 🌊 [Macro Trends & Vector Impact Analyst](./trends-analysis.md)
* **Filename:** [`trends-analysis.md`](./trends-analysis.md)
* **What it does:** Evaluates external macro forces operating over a 2-year horizon across three specific pillars: **Regulatory**, **Technological**, and **Demographic/Behavioral**. It classifies each trend as a 🚀 **Tailwind** or 💨 **Headwind** for your specific hypothesis and provides actionable strategic counter-measures and leading indicator tracking.

---

### 5. 🧪 [Customer Discovery & Interview Synthesis Strategist](./customer-discovery.md)
* **Filename:** [`customer-discovery.md`](./customer-discovery.md)
* **What it does:** A dual-purpose customer research engine. In **Question Audit mode**, it flags leading, future-facing, or social-desirability questions and rewrites them into past-behavior probes with deflection traps. In **Synthesis mode**, it analyzes single or multi-interview transcripts, produces supporting vs. refuting evidence balance sheets, and audits founder confirmation bias.

---

### 6. 🧩 [Idea Pattern & Anti-Pattern Synthesizer](./pattern-recognition.md)
* **Filename:** [`pattern-recognition.md`](./pattern-recognition.md)
* **What it does:** Conducts portfolio-level pattern recognition across a list of two or more ideas. It surfaces high-leverage shared moats and cross-pollination opportunities while exposing systemic structural traps (e.g., *Status-Quo Trap, API Tax, Double Cold-Start, Low-ACV/High-CAC*), ranking concepts by structural viability.

---

## 🎯 Recommended Workflow Sequence

1. **Ideation Portfolio Mapping:** Run [`pattern-recognition.md`](./pattern-recognition.md) to compare candidate concepts and eliminate structural traps early.
2. **Hypothesis Stress-Testing:** Pass your top candidate idea into [`pressure-test.md`](./pressure-test.md) to define falsifiable failure criteria.
3. **Competitive Red-Teaming:** Run [`competitor-analysis.md`](./competitor-analysis.md) to steelman competitor threats across all 4 tiers.
4. **Market & Buyer Sizing:** Use [`market-research.md`](./market-research.md) to build bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM models and map buyer vs. user decision flows.
5. **Macro Vector Analysis:** Use [`trends-analysis.md`](./trends-analysis.md) to evaluate 2-year regulatory, tech, and demographic tailwinds/headwinds.
6. **Customer Discovery Validation:** Draft interview questions and synthesize raw transcript notes using [`customer-discovery.md`](./customer-discovery.md) to audit confirmation bias and extract behavioral proof.
