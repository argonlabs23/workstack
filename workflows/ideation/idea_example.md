### 📝 The "Print and Pen" ERP Workaround
- **Date:** 2026-03-26
- **Industry:** `#Logistics`
- **Pain Point:** `#TooManual`, `#Fragmented`
- **Target Audience:** `#OperationsManagers`
- **Initial Rating (1-5):** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **The Breadcrumb (Observation):** 
  > *Visited a friend's mid-sized warehouse today. The operations managers are forced to use a legacy, clunky ERP system. Because the routing software doesn't talk to their accounting tool, they literally print out daily delivery spreadsheets, manually cross out completed runs with a red pen, and then a temp worker spends 2 hours at the end of every shift manually re-entering that data into QuickBooks. They hate it, but they "just accept it's the way it is." Massive, expensive bottleneck.*
