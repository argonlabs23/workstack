### ⚡ Initial Rating Scale (1-5)
*Gut-check rating before the "simmering phase."*
* **1/5:** Mild annoyance. Probably a "vitamin."
* **2/5:** Noticeable friction, but existing workarounds are okay.
* **3/5:** Solid problem. Wastes noticeable time or money.
* **4/5:** High friction. Actively costing the user significant resources. 
* **5/5:** Hair-on-fire problem. Extremely painful; users are actively desperately hacking together awful solutions.
